ZAMIK - how to open the installer

Zamik is a free gift and is not signed with a paid Apple developer certificate.
The first time you open the installer, macOS warns that it comes from an unidentified
developer. That happens only once, and only for the installer. The plugins it installs
work straight away, with no Terminal commands.

macOS 15 Sequoia and newer
  1. Double-click Zamik-Installer.pkg. When macOS warns you, click Done.
  2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down to Security. Next to the message about Zamik-Installer.pkg,
     click Open Anyway, then enter your password.
  4. The installer opens. Click Continue through the steps and Install.

macOS 10.13 High Sierra to macOS 14 Sonoma
  1. Right-click (or Control-click) Zamik-Installer.pkg and choose Open.
  2. Click Open in the warning.
  3. Click Continue through the steps and Install.

After installing, restart your music software.
  - Logic Pro: Audio Units > Zamik Audio > Zamik.
    If it is missing: Settings > Plug-in Manager, select Zamik, Reset & Rescan Selection.
  - REAPER, Ableton Live, Cubase, Bitwig, Studio One: use the VST3 version; rescan if needed.

The installer puts the plugins here (all users on this Mac):
  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Zamik.component
  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Zamik.vst3

To uninstall, delete those two files.

Works on macOS 10.13 High Sierra and later, on Intel and Apple silicon Macs.
