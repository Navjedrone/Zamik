Zamik is installed

Restart your music software so it finds Zamik.

  • Logic Pro: Zamik is under Audio Units › Zamik Audio › Zamik. If it is missing, open Settings › Plug-in Manager, select Zamik and click Reset & Rescan Selection.
  • REAPER, Ableton Live, Cubase, Bitwig, Studio One: rescan plugins if Zamik does not appear on its own.

Tip: ADVANCE RANGE sets how far audio can move earlier. Zamik reports that much latency to your DAW, so keep it as small as you need. If you only move audio later, set it to 0 ms.
