ZAMIK

Bit-transparent time shift · version __VERSION__

Zamik moves audio earlier or later by whole samples and does nothing else: no gain, no interpolation, no dither. On the normal path the output is bit-identical to the input, only shifted.

This installer puts the plugins in the main Library, available to every user on this Mac:

  • Audio Unit for Logic Pro, GarageBand and MainStage
  • VST3 for Ableton Live, REAPER, Cubase, Bitwig and Studio One

To install only one of them, click Customize on the Installation Type step.

Works on macOS 10.13 High Sierra and later, on Intel and Apple silicon Macs.
